Directions for a civilized cup

V60 / pour-over · Medium-fine

15 gCoffee
250 gWater
94°CTemperature
  1. Grind 15 g of coffee, medium-fine. Heat 250 g of water to about 94°C.
  2. Bloom with 45 g of water for 45 seconds. A gentle swirl is quite sufficient.
  3. Pour slowly to 150 g, then 250 g. Aim to finish near 2:45; adjust the grind to taste.

No scale? Use your usual filter-coffee recipe. A little experimentation is part of the gift.
