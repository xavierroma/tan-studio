  1. Set up:Rinse the paper filter with hot water; empty the server.
  2. Dose:Add 20 g freshly ground coffee. Level the bed and tare.
  3. Bloom:Start the timer. Pour 40 g water; wait 30 seconds.
  4. Pour:At 0:30, pour to 120 g total. Continue in 40-60 g pulses to 300 g. Keep the bed wet; finish near 2:30.

96°C

~4:00
Medium-coarse▼
FineCoarse
  1. Set up:Place the filter with three layers facing the spout. Rinse and empty the brewer.
  2. Dose:Add 30 g freshly ground coffee. Level the bed and tare.
  3. Bloom:Start the timer. Pour 60 g water; wait 45 seconds.
  4. Pour:Pour in circles to 300 g total. Let the water level drop, then continue to 500 g. Let drain near 4:00.
